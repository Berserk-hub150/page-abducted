404 — PAGE ABDUCTED

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open chrome://extensions in desktop Chrome.
3. Enable Developer mode.
4. Click Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json.
5. Open a regular website and click the extension in the puzzle menu.

Restart: replay. Select: click a word/image. Cursor: chase your pointer.
Finale: abduct the whole page. Restore: undo. Cinema: hide controls.
Exit cinema or Escape: bring controls back. X: close and restore.

Browser-protected pages are unsupported. Refreshing removes the effect.
First experimental version; browser compatibility testing is pending.

Source and instructions: https://github.com/Berserk-hub150/page-abducted
